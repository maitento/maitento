# 1. Get access

1. **[Sign up](https://app.maitento.com/signup)** with your email address, or with your GitHub, Google or Microsoft
   account. Your organisation gets its own **tenant**, on the [Free plan](../concepts/plans-and-billing.md): your
   agents, files and runs are visible only to its members. If you signed up with an email address, open the link we
   email you to confirm it. No email after a few minutes? Choose **Send the link again**, on the page you signed up
   from or when you try to sign in.
2. **Sign in** at [app.maitento.com](https://app.maitento.com).

Joining a colleague's tenant instead? Open the invitation link in your email: it takes you to the portal to choose a
password, and you join their tenant rather than getting your own.

The portal is where you can see everything: your agents and interactions, every run and its conversation, your files
and knowledge bases, usage, billing, and settings. You can write and deploy agents there too, in **Build studio**.

## Inviting your team

Tenant admins invite colleagues from **Settings → Users**. Everyone is either an **admin** or a **member**:

| | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Build and run agents, interactions, apps, capsules and pipelines | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create secrets, file systems, webhooks and automations | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create knowledge bases and upload documents to them | ✓ | |
| Add and change model keys | ✓ | |
| Manage users | ✓ | |
